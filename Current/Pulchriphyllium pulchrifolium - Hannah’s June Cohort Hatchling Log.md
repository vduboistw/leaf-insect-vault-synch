## Record structure and interpretation

- This report consolidates the historical log, subsequent observations, environmental screenshots, photograph assessments and dated corrections into one chronological table.
- Historical information has been retained. Retrospective confirmations and interpretations are explicitly distinguished from observations made on the date concerned.
- Daily coverage runs from 3 October through 5 November, including dates with no supplied observations.
- “No observation supplied” does not mean a health check occurred or that conditions were confirmed unchanged.
- Actual hatch times remain unknown except where explicitly reported.
- Survivor identities cannot currently be linked reliably to the original JN numbers.
- Absence of feeding marks or frass is recorded as “not observed,” rather than assumed to indicate feeding failure.
- No new deaths, hatchings or moults were supplied after the last dated reports. Their absence from the record must not be treated as proof that none occurred.

## Environmental data conventions

- Historical incubator values are visual estimates from supplied graphs, not exported sensor statistics. They are approximate for the visible intervals.
- Historical temperature and humidity figures are presented in the order **average / minimum / maximum**.
- Graph traces do not always cover a complete 24-hour period; available coverage information is retained in the table.
- Weather observations describe outside conditions and must not be confused with enclosure measurements.
- Forecasts remain explicitly labelled as forecasts, not observations.
- The newer screenshots show a sensor title truncated to “Pulchriphyllium …”. Its physical location is not established by those screenshots. Their readings are therefore kept in a separate column and are not attributed to the nursery or adult enclosure.
- Daily ranges from the newer screenshots describe the visible smoothed traces, not raw sensor extrema.
- Averages for those newer daily screenshots remain uncalculated: smoothed screenshots and irregularly spaced hour labels do not provide a reliable time-weighted daily mean.
- Exact tooltip readings are distinguished from visually estimated ranges.
- Where a trace extends below the screenshot boundary, its full minimum is unknown.
- October monthly-view values are daily plotted values with an unspecified aggregation method. They are not confirmed daily averages or extrema.
- Rough monthly-view readings have an estimated precision of approximately 0.5°C and 1–2 percentage points RH. Intraday minima and maxima cannot be recovered from that view.
- Monthly-view values do not replace the contemporaneous incubator readings or separately reported spot measurements.
