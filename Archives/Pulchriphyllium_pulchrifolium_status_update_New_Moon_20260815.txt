Pulchriphyllium pulchrifolium colony — New Moon status update
Checkpoint: 2026-08-15
Cycle covered: 2026-08-02 through 2026-08-15

The colony closes this half-cycle with three live adult females: Bethany, Deborah and Liza. No males remain. All three females remain active layers. The latest quantified July cohort count available in the project record is 46 eggs as of 2026-07-28. During Sam's caretaker period, daily egg output appeared broadly stable, but by 2026-08-13 he reported that newly laid eggs seemed progressively smaller than earlier eggs. This has not yet been measured or attributed to a particular female and should remain an observation rather than an interpretation.

Typhoon influence on 2026-08-09 brought a marked cooling to roughly 28–29°C, followed by very mild post-typhoon conditions on 2026-08-10 and higher humidity. Temperatures then returned gradually toward 29–31°C. A full-day enclosure dataset is missing for 2026-08-04 and is retained explicitly as a gap. Screenshot-derived values marked approximate in the source log remain approximate in the archive.

Established husbandry continued with guava as the primary food and wax apple available, plus active summer ventilation and humidity management as needed. Sam remained caretaker through 2026-08-15 while Vincent was away; Vincent resumes direct colony care from 2026-08-16. No confirmed moult, new individual, death/removal, or individually attributable abnormal behaviour occurred during this cycle, so the Individual Progression Table does not require a biological-status rewrite at this checkpoint.
