Pulchrifollium colony — New Moon status update
Checkpoint: 2026-07-15
Cycle covered: 2026-06-30 through 2026-07-14

The completed cycle opened with three surviving females: Bethany and Deborah as adults, and Liza in her final juvenile instar. Liza entered a prolonged pre-moult position in the penthouse “gym” and completed her imaginal moult successfully on 2026-07-06. The living colony therefore closes the cycle with three adult females and no surviving males.

Environmental conditions remained broadly stable and warm, generally around 28–30 °C. The lowest recorded enclosure humidity was approximately 51% on 2026-07-09 during hot, dry weather. Typhoon Bavi then brought warm, highly humid conditions, including some stagnant enclosure air and a heavier ageing-leaf odour. Drawbridge management, misting, partial or complete foliage changes, and selective ventilation were used as needed. Guava remained the primary food, supplemented with wax-apple. Some cut branches wilted or aged earlier than expected during and after the typhoon period.

Bethany began confirmed oviposition during the cycle, including a camera-recorded egg flick on 2026-07-08. Deborah subsequently also began laying. At cycle close, their current production is approximately one egg per day per actively laying adult female; detailed egg counts and future hatching records are maintained in the dedicated egg-care project. Liza is newly adult and has not yet reached her expected laying window.

Main watch points for the next cycle are: continued health and feeding of all three adult females; Liza’s adult maturation and eventual first oviposition; stable ventilation during hot and humid weather; foliage longevity and any recurrence of stale or near-decay odour; and high-level monitoring of the laying rate while detailed egg records remain separate.
