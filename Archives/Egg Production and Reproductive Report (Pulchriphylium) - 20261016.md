10. Data integrity and interpretation rules

11. Cohort total means eggs physically collected/recorded in that month, including later-discovered eggs. It does not guarantee all were laid in that month.
12. Recovered eggs: nine at the 18 September cleaning (date of laying uncertain); two at 30 September cleaning; two at 13 October relocation. The latter two are explicitly excluded from October short-interval fresh-collection rates; cleaning finds must not be treated as known same-day oviposition.
13. Individual attribution levels: witnessed/confirmed, strong positional inference, supported both-female activity, or unattributed. Do not upgrade inference to confirmation.
14. Missing entry (—) is unknown collection input, not evidence of zero laying. Unspecified morphology follows the regular-size/regular-colour log convention.
15. Monthly and individual-rate estimates use differing denominators and include collection-time biases. September 1.40 is a cohort-based estimate and not a direct count of eggs laid on each day.
16. Historical source preservation: The 25 September report and 9 October addendum are integrated here without treating the addendum’s retrospective snapshot totals as new eggs. New records begin 10 October. The original reports’ checkpoint totals (266 and 302) remain valid for their respective dates.
