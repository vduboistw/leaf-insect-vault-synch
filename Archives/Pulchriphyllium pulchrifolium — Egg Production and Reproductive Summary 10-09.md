Working hypotheses

- Seasonal cooling may reduce metabolic activity and egg-production rate.
- Lower relative humidity may contribute.
- Female ageing may influence production.
- Normal short-term variability remains possible.
- Dietary quality and composition remain relevant potential factors.

The environmental changes coincide with reduced average egg production, but causation is not established.

Egg production was already below September’s average during the warmer first days of October. Any delayed biological response remains unquantified.

No intervention is recommended solely to increase egg production while both females remain active and egg morphology remains normal.
