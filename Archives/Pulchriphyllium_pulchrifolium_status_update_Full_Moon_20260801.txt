Pulchriphyllium pulchrifolium — Full Moon checkpoint status update
Cycle covered: 2026-07-15 through 2026-08-01

The colony remains housed in the Summer Palace enclosure under the established summer ventilation and humidity-management routine. Food is supplied primarily as fresh guava, with wax apple available as a secondary branch. The enclosure was handed to Sam on the evening of 2026-07-22 after replacement of the floor towels and a complete refresh of guava and wax apple foliage.

Environmental conditions were generally stable. Enclosure temperature stayed close to 29–30°C on most days, with one brief overnight spike to approximately 33.5°C on 2026-07-26. Humidity was usually in the 60–70% range, but management became more difficult during prolonged air-conditioning use. The lowest recorded point of the cycle was approximately 56% on 2026-08-01. Fresh foliage repeatedly prompted immediate feeding, indicating that branches can become less acceptable before visible wilting is obvious.

The live population is Bethany, Deborah and Liza, all adult females; no males remain. All three are now laying. The July egg cohort had reached 46 eggs by 2026-07-28. Deborah was directly confirmed as the source of distinctly pale or white eggs during a short episode, accompanied by some pale, small, hard frass containing a larger crystalline component. The most recent eggs returned to the usual dark-brown shell colour. Liza's first adult mango feeding was confirmed by video and feeding marks, and her onset of oviposition was supported by three excess eggs and ovipositor pigmentation.

Watch points for the next cycle are: preventing prolonged humidity dips during AC use; replacing foliage according to insect preference rather than appearance alone; monitoring whether Deborah produces further pale eggs or pale frass; tracking the three females' laying rate at high level; and continuing the dedicated egg-care follow-up for fertility and future hatching outcomes.
